Meet The Resilience Architect

Santiago "Santi" Strasser is a Resilience Architect – a leader who helps individuals and teams build trust, purpose, and emotional endurance. With a foundation in holistic thinking and emotional intelligence, Santi guides others to close resilience gaps by aligning their values, goals, and actions.

Born in Argentina and now based in the Boston Area, Santi's journey has always been about transformation: from navigating language barriers to leading with vulnerability. His work draws on real-world leadership experience, creating frameworks that empower people to reflect deeply, lead authentically, and connect meaningfully.

As a speaker, mentor, and facilitator, Santi supports emerging leaders, entrepreneurs, and organizations in cultivating cultures of psychological safety and sustained growth. He is passionate about crafting safe playgrounds for innovation – where failure is just a step forward, and relationships are the fabric for long-term resilience.
